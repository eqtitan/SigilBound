## Animations: `anims_ual.json`

Seven clips (Spell_Simple_Enter / Shoot / Exit / Idle_Loop, Hit_Chest, Hit_Head, Death01) from
**Quaternius, Universal Animation Library (Standard / free tier)**, CC0 1.0.
Original pages: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html and
https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library.

The source files came from a GitHub mirror that redistributes the free CC0 tier unmodified:
https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library (commit e24c23cf, 2025-06-10), with the
pack's CC0 LICENSE file. **Before a store release, re-download from quaternius.com or itch and
confirm the clips match.**

The clips are retargeted from the pack's Rigify (`DEF-*`) rig onto our character rig and baked to
JSON by `tools/anim-retarget` (rotations for 20 bones, plus hips and feet positions; 24 fps).

## Image-to-3D hero meshes (new heroes, 2026-11-27)

Base meshes for Vorgrim Ashscale, Talvo Brassfang, Wystan Quillmere, Crusk Brineclaw and Hartwin
Sunhorn were generated by the owner from the owner's own concept art with **TRELLIS** (Microsoft,
MIT license; https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS) on the public Hugging Face Space
`trellis-community/TRELLIS` (free ZeroGPU tier). The concepts and the resulting meshes are the
project's own art; TRELLIS is a tool, not a content source. The meshes are cleaned, rigged to the
UBC skeleton and skinned in Blender (`unity/Tools~/blenderforge`). Any mesh made with another
generator (e.g. Hunyuan3D or Tripo) must have its tool, licence and any attribution added here
before it ships.

Later meshes (2026-11-27) for Crusk Brineclaw (regenerated), Nyssa Duskwing, Vessa Silkmourne,
Brannoc, Vex, Aurex, Odo and 27 more roster heroes (remodelled to match their portraits) and the Titan were generated from
front images the owner approved through fal.ai's hosted **`fal-ai/trellis`** endpoint, which runs the
same MIT-licensed TRELLIS model as a paid API call; the outputs are the project's own art on the same
terms as above.

Remodels 2026-11-28: Wystan Quillmere (tunic robe) and Vorgrim Ashscale (half-cloak) from new front images made
with Google Gemini (owner-approved prompts) and turned into meshes with fal.ai `fal-ai/trellis` (MIT model), same terms.

## Spring bones (code)

Skirts, robes and coat tails on some high-detail heroes swing on spring-bone chains simulated at runtime by
`Core/SpringSolver.cs`. Its algorithm (Verlet tail, stiffness pull to the animated rest direction, drag, gravity,
length restore, sphere/capsule push-out, then rotate the bone onto the tail) follows **UniVRM**'s
`VRMSpringBone` (https://github.com/vrm-c/UniVRM), **MIT licence**, Copyright (c) 2020 VRM Consortium,
Copyright (c) 2018 Masataka SUMI for MToon. The C# here is our own re-implementation (no UniVRM source files are
included); the chains and weights are added to the meshes by `unity/Tools~/blenderforge/spring_rig.py`.
