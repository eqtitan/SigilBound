# Sigilbound privacy policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026. Applies to the Sigilbound app for Android and Windows ("Sigilbound (Unity)")._

Sigilbound is a single-player fantasy hero game with optional friends and chat. This page explains what the game stores and sends.

## What stays on your device

- **Your game save**: heroes, progress, currencies, gear and settings. It is stored only on your device (the app's local storage). We never receive it.
- **Device settings**: graphics quality, frame rate, screen shake, camera view, tutorial progress and which "What's new" notes you have seen.
- **Feedback reports**: if you use *Settings → Send feedback*, the report is saved on your device (`feedback.txt`) and copied to your clipboard. It is only sent if **you** paste it into a message to us. It contains your note, the game build, your device model, operating system, graphics settings and recent error messages from the game.

## What is sent, and only if you use Friends

Friends and chat are optional. If you open **Friends** and choose a player name, the game talks to our server (`sigilbound.pages.dev`) and stores there:

- your **player name** and a random **friend code** and account token (no email, phone number or real name);
- a small **public profile** shown to your friends: stages cleared, team power, best Titan damage, number of heroes and your top heroes;
- your **friend list**, friend requests and **blocked players**;
- **chat messages** you send to friends, and any **reports** you file about a message.

You can **delete your Friends account** at any time in the Friends screen; this removes your name, profile, friends, blocks and messages from the server.

## Updates (versions installed outside an app store)

Builds downloaded from our GitHub page check `api.github.com` for a newer version and download it from GitHub. This sends no personal information beyond what any web request includes (your IP address, seen by GitHub). Store builds update through the store instead.

## What we don't do

- No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no in-app purchases.
- We don't sell or share data with third parties. The server runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests on our behalf.
- The game doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

## Children

Sigilbound is not directed at children under 13. Chat is between friends who add each other by code, and every message can be reported and its sender blocked.
