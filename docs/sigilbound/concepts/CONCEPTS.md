# Sigilbound hero concepts: the 100-hero rebuild

Concept designs for the owner's review. Every hero keeps their name, element, role, rarity and skills. Each one is rebuilt as a high-detail 3D model on the Quaternius Universal Base Characters (regular male or female body) with the Modular Fantasy outfit pieces, plus custom props we model ourselves. The machine-readable version for the modellers is `concepts.json` in the same folder.

## Style in brief

- **Warm, hand-painted fantasy.** Chunky, readable shapes and saturated colours, not gritty. Villains look villainous through posture and colour, never gore.
- **One idea per hero at phone size.** In battle a hero is about 70 pixels tall, so each design has one silhouette idea (a giant anchor, a candle helm, glider wings), one main colour, one accent colour and a prop that says what they do.
- **The element shows as light.** Flame `#e8553a`, Gale `#3fb57a`, Tide `#3a8ee8`, Light `#e8c23a` and Shadow `#9a5ae8` appear in each hero's rim light, glowing details and skill effects, so matchups read at a glance.
- **Role shows in the shape.** Attackers lean forward with long diagonal weapons; Defenders are wide and square behind shields; HP heroes are round and heavy; Supports are tall and flowing, with a light or instrument held high.
- **Rarity shows as finish.** Commons wear practical gear; Rares add one signature piece; Epics add capes, ornaments and a few glowing runes; Legendaries add something above the head (crown, halo, flame, wings or a floating object) and an aura.
- **No look-alikes.** All 39 near-twin pairs from the uniqueness audit are fixed. Each hero now has a different prop and a signature that no one else has (summary table below).
- **Original only.** Every design comes from the hero's own name, skills and story. Nothing imitates another game's characters.
