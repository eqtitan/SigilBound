## 2. Bugs (most severe first)

1. **S2: The Titan model is mangled by its new clips.**
   - **Where:** every Titan battle at every size (78-titan-intro to 84-titan-6).
   - **What I saw:** limbs stretched into long polygon sheets and spikes; in titan-1 the stretched mesh fills a third of the frame. In the portrait intro the Titan hovers in the air in a spread pose above the ground.
   - **Expected:** the hunched basalt colossus from builds 176 and 180, knuckle-walking and slamming.
   - **Likely cause:** the 11 new baked Titan clips (`titan.json` clips) don't match the skinned mesh's binding. Every structural test passes and `skinned=4` looks healthy to the smoke gate, so only the picture shows it.
   - **Impact:** the daily boss is the first thing every player sees in Trials and looks broken. Its damage and rewards are unaffected.
   - **Evidence:** `titan-before-f7ddd6b-left-after-right.jpg`, `titan-all-shots-2400.jpg`, `titan-all-shots-portrait.jpg`.
   - **Suggested permanent check:** compare the Titan's on-screen bounds per shot against a stored range; stretched skinning blows the bounds out.
2. **S2: NullReferenceException in `ArenaReactor.Push`.**
   - **When:** in portrait (1080x2400), in both portrait runs, between the story-storm-6 and story-glass-6 battles.
   - **Stack:** `ArenaReactor.Push` ← `OnShock` ← `ArenaEvents.RaiseShock` ← `BattleController.DoAct`.
   - **Cause:** the reactor subscribes to shock events in `OnEnable` but only allocates its `_w`/`_cw` arrays in `Start`. A shock raised in the gap dereferences null. The gap is old, but this build exposes it: ultimates and boss blows now raise shocks in every arena battle (`BattleController.cs:733`, and `:695` for the Titan slam; both new since build 180).
   - **Impact:** `DoAct` is a nested iterator inside the battle loop, so the exception ends that battle's coroutine. In the tour the next battle carried on normally, so the coroutine that died was most likely the battle being left, and I saw no stuck battle. Still, the project's smoke rule (`tools/unity-smoke-check.sh`) fails this tour; CI passed only because its single run didn't hit the gap.
   - **Fix suggestion:** allocate in `Awake`, or null-guard `Push`.
   - **Evidence:** `portrait-nullreference.txt`, `portrait-story-around-exception.jpg`.
3. **S3: A second Titan fight in the same session starts with its health bar drained and its ridges broken.**
   - **Cause:** `BattleController._titanShown` (the damage shown on the Titan's bar) is only ever added to (`BattleController.cs:759`). `Begin()` clears `_taken`/`_healed` but not this field, and one BattleController lives for the whole session (`App.cs:76`).
   - **Steps:** practice the Titan, then fight it for real (or fight it twice without restarting). The second fight's bar starts where the first ended, and on the first hit `TitanBody.SetHealth` breaks the ridges straight to that stage.
   - **Impact:** visual only; damage and rewards come from the battle itself.
   - **Found by:** code review, so not seen on screen. The tour fights the Titan once.
   - **Suggested test:** begin two Titan battles and check `_titanShown == 0` at the second `Begin`.
4. **S3: "Larger tap targets" makes the portrait battle buttons overlap on real phones.**
   - **Where:** in portrait, stage battles draw Leave plus six round buttons (Pause, speed, Auto, Replay, Climb, View) across a 390-unit row (`BattleController.cs:1228`).
   - **The arithmetic:** on a 1080-wide, 430 dpi phone, 48 dp works out to about 46 units, so the row needs about 384 units and fits. At 56 dp it's about 54 units, so the row needs about 426: View lands on top of Leave and the stage label.
   - **Why the tours can't show it:** headless runs report 96 dpi, so `Ui.Tap` stays at its 44-unit floor even with the option seeded (confirmed in the seeded tour).
   - **Fix suggestion:** wrap or shrink the row when it doesn't fit.
5. **S4: At Extra-large text, the portrait bottom bar labels run together** ("BATTLE HEROESSUMMON TRIALS FRIENDS"). Evidence: `a11y-nav-labels-run-together.jpg`.
6. **S4: Some colour-blind palette colours are below 4.5:1 on the dark panel** where they colour text: the test reports a lowest of 3.2:1. They pass as colour swatches but are thin as text.
7. **S4: The Titan's health bar empties at 220k damage while it keeps fighting.** The bar is now "what's left of the top tier". About 13% of maxed teams reach 220k, after which they fight an empty-barred Titan until turn 20. Consider a "tier cleared" state.
8. **S4:** the stray prop in front of Tansy; Nell's piece and the specks around Fennick, Brega and Holt are still visible (see section 1).

## 2c. Exploratory findings

1. **Read what changed** (about 2,300 lines across 30 code files). The risky areas were the Titan clip and body runtime, the spring solver, accessibility scaling in every UI helper, the Titan bar state, and the new shock events. Reading code found **bug 3** (bar state never reset) and **bug 4** (56 dp row overflow), and explained **bug 2** (the shock gap).
2. **Accessibility tour without touching code.**
   - **How:** the Linux player keeps PlayerPrefs in `~/.config/unity3d/unknown/unknown/prefs`. I backed it up, added `sb.textSize=3`, `sb.palette=deutan`, `sb.flash=off`, `sb.reduceMotion=1` and `sb.bigTaps=1`, ran a 1080x2400 tour, then restored the file.
   - **Result:** DONE 84. Text is bigger everywhere and shrinks to fit (Home quests, campaign, Settings). Red-green colours apply: the orange badge and the blue level-up lines. Found **bug 5**. The run also reproduced **bug 2**.
   - **Suggestion (no code changed):** a `-sbPrefs=textSize=3,palette=deutan` argument for ShotsMode, or this prefs-file seeding in `tools/unity-shots.sh`, would make an accessibility tour a one-liner. Note that the prefs file is shared between runs (it holds `sb.changelogSeen` and `sb.act2Intro` from earlier tours), so tours aren't fully independent today.
3. **Titan comparison against build 180.** I put the same shots side by side, which is how **bug 1** surfaced. The structural Titan tests (42 lines) don't look at the picture.
4. **Spring solver.** The SpringTests already cover uneven frames, long gaps, catch-up limits, collision and cost. I added nothing and found nothing.
5. **Balance and rules.** The battle rules didn't change except the Titan presentation and spring data. All probes are identical to build 180, and the Auto Climb rules still pass their tests (I ran my 260-climb simulation on `648aa4b`, whose climb rules are the same apart from the wording fix).

**Recommended permanent tests:** Titan screen-bounds check (bug 1); reactor shock before `Start` (bug 2); two Titan `Begin`s (bug 3); a portrait HUD row-fit check at 56 dp and 480 dpi (bug 4); a bottom-bar label fit check at Extra large (bug 5).
