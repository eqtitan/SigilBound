## 2. Bugs (most severe first)

1. **S2: The new heroes reshuffled every Act II stage and undid the Act II rebalance for mid-strength teams.**
   - **Where:** campaign Act II (stages 11-1 to 15-6, Normal and Hard).
   - **What I saw:** `Game.StageWaves` draws Act II enemies from *every* hero (`var pool = idx < ClassicStages ? Classic : Heroes;`) by index. Adding seven heroes changed the line-up of every Act II stage. The `GameData.Classic` comment says "New heroes join the enemy pools deliberately, later", but that only protects Act I.
   - **Campaign probe, mid team** (strong team in brackets):

     | Stage | Build 175 | Build 176 |
     |---|---|---|
     | 12-6 | 70% | **3%** |
     | 14-6 | 95% | **0%** (93% strong) |
     | 15-5 | 65% | **23%** |
     | 14-2 | 68% | 35% |
     | 13-5 | 100% | 50% |
     | 15-4 | 98% | 65% |
     | Act II Light/Shadow overall | 82.5% | **62.3%** (4 stages under 50%) |
     | Act II triangle overall | 90.8% | 88.2% |

     The strong team is still fine (75% or better everywhere).
   - **Expected:** this build's own changelog says "Act II climbs more gently to a beatable final boss", and the live-tester checklist asks them to find walls.
   - **Impact:** players partway through Act II will meet different enemies, and three new walls, after updating. Nothing is lost.
   - **Fix options:** pin Act II to the pre-release roster (like `Classic`), or re-run the step-99 tuning with the new pool.
   - **Evidence:** `evidence/902e814/campaign-act2-diff.txt`.
   - **Suggested permanent test:** a golden list of each stage's enemy ids, so a roster change can't move stages unnoticed.
2. **S3 (carried over): the portrait battle HUD and campaign list didn't get the controls patch.** Small 34-unit buttons, no Pause, no ally/enemy glyphs on the turn chips. Re-confirmed in `evidence/902e814/portrait-battle-hud-top.jpg`. Details are in the build 175 report.
3. **S3 (carried over): Undo after a bulk release puts saved-team heroes back in swapped order.** Same repro on this build: saved team `[8,9]`, release both, undo, the team is `[9,8]`.
4. **S4: The Titan intro doesn't show the Titan's face.** At 2400x1080 and 1080x2400 the opening shot is Pell's back filling the frame, with the Titan's head hidden behind him. In the Quake shot (titan-5) the camera is so close that the Titan's name tag sits on top of the "QUAKE" banner. Evidence: `titan-intro-2400.jpg`, `titan-quake-close-2400.jpg`.
5. **S4: Gallery and Home line-ups crowd the wider new heroes.** Vorgrim's sword and wings run through Talvo, and Wystan, Crusk, Hartwin and Nyssa overlap each other on gallery page 39. Battles use the new slot widths and look fine. Evidence: `gallery-vorgrim-talvo-overlap.jpg`, `gallery-wystan-crusk-hartwin-nyssa.jpg`.
6. **S4 (carried over):**
   - stage-row enemy names are cut off (for example "Tamsin Lv");
   - the portrait Home hides the team (Vessa too);
   - portrait locked chapter cards are see-through;
   - the release review "!" under-warns.
7. **S4 (code):** the unused-variable warning in `RankUpPanel.cs(62)` is still there.

**Advisory:** the Titan got easier to max with the new heroes in the pool. Average damage is 49,793 → 52,557; the maxed team's 140k tier reach went 31% → 39% and the 220k tier 3% → 13%. It is still in range. It means the Radiant shard tier is now realistic for a maxed team.

## 2c. Exploratory findings

1. **Read what changed.** I read `git log a6d2fee..902e814` and the diff (1,215 lines across 27 code files). Risky areas: new hero data entering existing pools, save compatibility with new hero ids, the battle layout for wider creatures, and the Titan camera.
   - **Pools:** I traced every use of the full hero list. Act I, dungeons, Trials and rivals use `Classic` (24 heroes, unchanged). **Act II and summons use the full list.** The Act II side effect is **bug 1**, found this way and confirmed with the campaign probe.
2. **Saves with new heroes on older builds.** The test "an unknown hero is kept aside, not loaded or dropped", plus a write-back test, covers a save holding Vessa or the new heroes being loaded by an older build. Nothing found.
3. **Battles at the extremes, including the new heroes and Vessa.** Every hero alone at Lv1 1★ and Lv60 6★ against four random enemies: 471 battles, no exception, no stalemate.
4. **Roster tools re-run on this core.** Release fuzz over 1,500 rosters and Rank-up-all fuzz over 1,500 plans (6,060 rank-ups) give the same result as build 175: everything holds except the known undo-order bug (3 rosters).
5. **Screens at three sizes with the creature hero.** Vessa fits the Home line-up and the battle slots at 1280x720, 2400x1080 and 1080x2400. In portrait the Home team is still hidden (carried over). I ran no extra odd-size tour: the three standard sizes covered everything that changed, with no layout code touched apart from the line-up spacing.
6. **Consistency hunt.**
   - Changelog vs screen: the Titan intro does not show its face (bug 4). The Gale/Light/Shadow medallions can't be seen in the tour (not verified).
   - Codex now reads 3/157 (150 + 7). Correct.
   - The `GameData.Classic` comment says new heroes join the enemy pools "later", while the Act II code already uses them (bug 1).
7. **Budgets.**
   - The new Titan is about 9% lighter: 93-95k triangles vs about 103k.
   - The caldera adds renderers in the Titan shots (+25% to +139% depending on the camera); the triangle totals still fell.
   - The Vessa battle line-up is 177k triangles, versus 133k for a normal 4-hero battle. Vessa costs about 44k more than an average hero. No hard limit was broken, but it is worth watching on low-end phones.

**Recommended permanent tests:** a golden enemy list per campaign stage (bug 1); a Titan-intro framing check that the head is visible in the first shot (bug 4); gallery and line-up spacing using `Creatures.SlotWidth` for wide heroes (bug 5).
