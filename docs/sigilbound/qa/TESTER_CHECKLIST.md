# Sigilbound: what to try in this build

Install or update from the usual link:
https://github.com/eqtitan/SigilBound/releases/download/unity-latest/Sigilbound-Unity.apk
(Android may ask to allow installs from this source. After the first install the game offers updates itself.)

Please send back: your phone model, what you tried, and anything that looked wrong, felt slow or sounded off.
A screenshot helps a lot. Settings → Send feedback also copies a report with your device and recent errors.

## 1. NEW in this build: screens and buttons (please check these first)
- [ ] **Home:** quests on the left, your team in the middle, and a **Continue Campaign** button on the right
      (or above the bottom bar in portrait). Is anything cut off, overlapping or covered by your phone's camera hole?
- [ ] **Buttons are bigger.** Can you hit everything easily with a thumb? Any button still too small?
- [ ] **Stage list** (open a chapter on the map): each stage is one big row. Does a tap always open the stage you touched?
      Tapping a locked stage should tell you what to clear first.
- [ ] **Normal | Hard** and **Act I | Act II** are now switches at the top of the campaign map. Easy to find and use?
- [ ] **Pressing a button** should make it dip slightly and click. Do all buttons react?
- [ ] **Greyed-out buttons** (things you can't do yet) should still be readable. Any you can't read?
- [ ] **Colour-blind check:** enemies and allies in the turn bar show a skull or a shield, elements show icons,
      and rarity frames show a letter (C / R / E / L). Damage numbers say WEAK! or RESIST.
- [ ] Rounded screen corners or a notch: does anything sit under them?

## 2. NEW: fairer campaign and clearer battles
- [ ] Light and Shadow chapters, stage 10-1 and the end of Act II should now be beatable with a decent team.
      Still a wall somewhere? Tell us the stage number and your team.
- [ ] A **Round** counter sits under the turn bar. From round 10 it turns red with **Enrage** (damage up, healing down).
      Did you notice it? Is it readable?
- [ ] **Broken** now has its own look: cracked-sigil icon, a recoil and an orange "BREAK!" banner. Clearly different from Stun?
- [ ] The dodge animation should only play when a hit does no damage.
- [ ] The victory screen's **Healing** column should show real numbers now.
- [ ] Auto battle should finish off weak enemies more cleverly.

## 3. NEW: phone performance and safety
- [ ] The game now runs at **60 fps** by default (Settings lets you pick 30, 60 or your screen's 90/120).
      Auto-replay runs at 30 to save battery. Is it smooth? Does your phone get warm?
- [ ] The screen can now go to sleep in menus (it stays awake in battle). Any problems?
- [ ] Long sessions should no longer slow down or crash. Play for 20+ minutes and tell us if it does.
- [ ] Your progress is now backed up automatically. If the game ever says your save is damaged, please screenshot
      the message before tapping anything.
- [ ] **Titan and Arena:** a try is now used when the fight starts, so quitting mid-fight no longer gives a free retry.
      (Closing the app during an Arena fight counts as a loss.) Practice mode and the campaign are unchanged.

## 4. Campaign and story (Battle tab)
- [ ] Does each stage have a place name, and does the scenery change from stage to stage as you travel?
- [ ] Boss stages (stage 6) start with a short camera reveal of the boss's lair. Does a tap skip it?

## 5. Battles
- [ ] Do hits and spells look and sound right? Any effect that looks broken or too busy?
- [ ] Poison and Burn can stack up to x3 (small badge on the status icon). Readable?
- [ ] If a hero you use now feels useless or overpowered after the rebalance, tell us which.

## 6. The Titan (Trials tab)
- [ ] The arrival intro, its three moves and the final blow. Practice mode costs no tries.
- [ ] The Titan's new moves: Bedrock Hammer (it walks up on its fists and slams both down on one hero), Faultline Leap (it crouches, leaps and lands on your team) and Heartfire Torrent (fire pours from its glowing chest, not its mouth). Do the damage numbers pop the moment the fists or the fire land?
- [ ] Before each big move its seams and chest glow brighter and its back crystals light up. Do crystals break off when its health bar passes 66% and 33%? On Low graphics there should be no flying shards.
- [ ] If your team survives all 20 turns, does the Titan sink to its knees at the end?
- [ ] Reward tiers are now 15k / 40k / 80k / 140k / 220k damage. How far does your team get?

## 7. Heroes
- [ ] Any hero whose portrait or 3D model looks wrong (floating hair, odd blocks, stretched parts)? Tell us the name.
      (New 3D models for many heroes are on the way, so don't worry about animal heroes looking human for now.)
