# Sigilbound "What's new" (player-facing; newest first). Tags: new, fix, change, balance.
# Keep lines short and friendly; this is shown in the game after each update.

## 2026-11-25 | Heroines and a smoother updater
new: Frame rate "Auto" matches your screen (up to 120 Hz on phones that support it); 60 and 30 are still in Settings.
new: Screen shake setting: Full, Reduced or Off.
change: Screen shake looks the same at any frame rate.
fix: On Samsung phones, the update banner now explains Auto Blocker ("Unknown app blocked") and where to turn it off.
new: Forty heroines now have their own bodies: knights and rogues in hooded leathers, witches and mages in flowing dresses, and rangers in light gear.
new: A "What's new" window like this one appears after every update. Find it again any time in Settings.
fix: In-app updates on Android work again ("There's a problem with the app file").
fix: If an update can't install, the banner now says why instead of failing quietly.
change: The newest updates download faster to check, and the game always picks the latest build.

## 2026-11-24 | Act II: The Drowned Coast
new: Act II opens after chapter 10: five new chapters from Saltreach Harbor to the Storm Coast, each with its own story and scenery.
new: The world map has an Act I / Act II switch.
new: The roster grows to 100 heroes, each with a baked 3D model.
new: New combat effects: Burn, Pull, Counter and Revive, plus piercing, executing and rage skills.
balance: New heroes were tested against the original heroes of the same rarity and role.
fix: Battle backgrounds no longer flicker when the graphics quality adjusts itself.
fix: Heroes no longer float above the battlefield or walk on stilts.
