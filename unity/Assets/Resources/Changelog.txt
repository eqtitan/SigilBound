# Sigilbound "What's new" (player-facing; newest first). Tags: new, fix, change, balance.
# Keep lines short and friendly; this is shown in the game after each update.
# Start a NEW "## date | title" entry for each batch of changes (the game remembers which entries were seen).

## 2026-11-26 | Titan framing
change: Worm's-eye now frames the Titan with its name and health bars above it.
change: Meteor Leap: slow motion as the Titan leaps while the view widens to follow it to the top; then full speed and a tight view as it crashes down.

## 2026-11-26 | Smoother on Low
change: Low graphics quality is lighter on older phones: simpler hero shading and skinning.

## 2026-11-26 | Status icons
new: Buffs and debuffs now show as icons under each health bar (gold rims for buffs, violet for debuffs) with the turns left. Tap them to see what each one means.

## 2026-11-26 | Titan camera rework
change: Worm's-eye now sits just behind your team, low and looking up, with your heroes in the foreground and the Titan towering behind them.
change: Meteor Leap: the camera zooms out to take in the Titan's whole leap, fall and slam, then zooms back in.

## 2026-11-26 | Every hero remade
new: Every hero has been rebuilt with real modelled weapons and shields, flowing gowns, proper wings, crowns, horns, hats, masks, veils and accessories from their designs.
new: Hand-painted shading on every hero: soft shadows in folds and worn, brighter edges.

## 2026-11-26 | Ready for hero portraits
new: Heroes can now show painted portraits on the hero list and in the summon reveal (they arrive as the art is made).

## 2026-11-26 | Titan practice
new: Practice against the Titan as often as you like from the Titan screen: no rewards and no daily try used.

## 2026-11-26 | The fallen stay down
fix: Fallen heroes and enemies now stay where they fell instead of sinking out of sight.
fix: The smaller-download change now actually shrinks the Android download.

## 2026-11-26 | Hollow enemies in high detail
new: Hollow enemies now use the heroes' high-detail models, as dark violet shadows with a crimson glow.

## 2026-11-26 | The Titan, colossal
new: The Titan now towers over your team, far bigger than before.
new: Three Titan-only camera views on the View button: Worm's-eye (looking up past your team, the new default), Side duel and over the Titan's shoulder.
new: Titan fights open with an arrival: the camera climbs the Titan from its feet to its burning visor as it roars. Tap to skip.
new: The Titan's ultimate is now the Meteor Leap: it bounds out of sight and crashes down on your team.
new: Its fight-ending blow is the Earthsplitter: a slow-motion overhead slam that cracks the ground.
change: On the Titan's turns the camera dips and looks up at it as it winds up (less with Reduced screen shake, none with Off).

## 2026-11-26 | Steady lakes
fix: Lakes behind the arena no longer flicker in and out of view.

## 2026-11-26 | Titan in view
fix: In Titan fights every camera view now keeps the Titan in full view below the turn bar and skill name.

## 2026-11-26 | Signature effects
new: Every hero now has a signature effect from their design: embers, wind swirls, splashes, petals, lightning, wisps, notes and more, on every hit and big on ultimates.
new: Melee strikes leave a glowing trail in the hero's element colour.
new: Legendary heroes carry a faint aura of their signature effect.

## 2026-11-26 | Painted buttons
new: Buttons are now hand-painted gold, violet and crimson plates with jewelled ornaments. Prefer the old look? Settings > Painted interface > Classic.

## 2026-11-26 | Counters and body language
new: Heroes with Counter now turn and strike back at their attacker.
new: Heroes stand taller when buffed, sag their shoulders when weakened, and tremble while poisoned or burning.

## 2026-11-26 | A much smaller download
change: The game download is a lot smaller: hero models are now packed tighter, with no change in how they look.

## 2026-11-26 | Heroes move again
fix: High-detail heroes no longer stand frozen with their arms out; they breathe, run, strike and react again (the Titan too).
fix: The Titan's name tag no longer covers the skill name at the top of the screen.

## 2026-11-26 | Combat stances
new: Heroes take their combat stance when their turn starts: archers draw, brawlers raise their fists, casters gather power.
new: Stuns from heavy weapons, the Titan or ultimates knock the target to the ground before they stagger back up.
change: Idle breathing no longer speeds up at 2x and 3x battle speed; only the action does.

## 2026-11-26 | A new Titan
new: The Titan is now a colossal horned stone guardian with a giant maul; its visor and chest rune burn in its current element's colour.

## 2026-11-26 | Heroes move again (for real)
fix: High-detail heroes play their animations instead of standing frozen with their arms out.

## 2026-11-26 | Fifty new heroes and sharper models
new: Forty more new heroes join the summons, for a roster of 150: elves, gnomes, beastfolk, golems, a barrow wraith and more.
change: Every high-detail hero was rebuilt: weapons sit in the hand, shields are strapped to the forearm, crowns and halos sit on the head, robes flow like cloth.
change: Heroes now have their own sizes: gnomes stand short, golems tall and broad.
balance: Every new hero was tested in thousands of simulated battles against the original hero of its rarity and role.

## 2026-11-26 | High-detail heroes move again
fix: High-detail heroes no longer stand frozen with their arms out: their animations play again.

## 2026-11-26 | Spear thrusts, heavy hits and fairer defeats
new: Spear and polearm heroes attack with a real lunge and thrust.
new: Heavy hits drop a hero to one knee before they rise.
change: The kneel-and-slump defeat now plays only when you lose an Arena (PvP) battle; heroes who fall in battle use the death animation.
change: Concept weapons and shields are held in the hand and on the forearm.

## 2026-11-25 | Battle feel, frame rate and a clearer camera
new: High-detail heroes move by weapon type: daggers, bows, casters, shields and brawlers each have their own stance and attacks; defenders block, and the losing team shows it.
new: Hits land on contact and freeze for a split second (hit-stop), so attacks feel heavier, at any frame rate.
fix: Low camera views (Cinematic) no longer get stuck behind or inside rocks and trees: scenery in the way is hidden.
new: Test: Brannoc, Lumen, Morwenna and Aurelia appear as the first high-detail models (work in progress).
new: Frame rate "Auto" matches your screen (up to 120 Hz on phones that support it); 60 and 30 are still in Settings.
new: Screen shake setting: Full, Reduced or Off.
change: Screen shake looks the same at any frame rate.
fix: On Samsung phones, the update banner now explains Auto Blocker ("Unknown app blocked") and where to turn it off.
fix: The "What's new" window shows again after updates.
new: Defeated heroes on the losing side now fall to their knees and slump forward.
new: Saved by Revive, a hero buckles toward the ground and rises back up; stunned heroes slump, dazed.
change: Multi-hit skills land each hit on its own beat; arrows and spells fly at a steady speed; knockback snaps out and eases back.

## 2026-11-25 | Heroines and a smoother updater
new: Forty heroines now have their own bodies: knights and rogues in hooded leathers, witches and mages in flowing dresses, and rangers in light gear.
new: A "What's new" window like this one appears after every update. Find it again any time in Settings.
fix: In-app updates on Android work again ("There's a problem with the app file").
fix: If an update can't install, the banner now says why instead of failing quietly.
change: The newest updates download faster to check, and the game always picks the latest build.

## 2026-11-24 | Act II: The Drowned Coast
new: Act II opens after chapter 10: five new chapters from Saltreach Harbor to the Storm Coast, each with its own story and scenery.
new: The world map has an Act I / Act II switch.
new: The roster grows to 100 heroes, each with a baked 3D model.
new: New combat effects: Burn, Pull, Counter and Revive, plus piercing, executing and rage skills.
balance: New heroes were tested against the original heroes of the same rarity and role.
fix: Battle backgrounds no longer flicker when the graphics quality adjusts itself.
fix: Heroes no longer float above the battlefield or walk on stilts.
