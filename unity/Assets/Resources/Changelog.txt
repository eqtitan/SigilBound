## 2026-11-28 | Clearer battles
fix: Skill pillars, heals and shields are now soft see-through light that fades out at the top, so you can always see who they hit.
fix: Spell circles are the size of the caster's floor disc instead of a big stamp over the neighbours.
fix: Damage numbers float above the name and health bar instead of over the body, spread a little, stack when several land at once and stay on screen.
change: WEAK! and RESIST sit on their own line under the number, with a dark shadow so they read on any background.
fix: Everyone stands inside the rune circle, allies on the near side and enemies on the far side; the circle grows a little for wide heroes like Vorgrim and Nyssa.
fix: Hollow enemies have a clean red outline and glowing red eyes instead of pink speckles.
fix: Boss-stage braziers are iron fire bowls with glowing embers and a small flame, not glowing lamp shades.

## 2026-11-27 | Smaller download

## 2026-11-27 | Lighter on memory
fix: The game no longer keeps every hero you have seen in memory: heroes not on screen are let go (the last few stay for quick reuse), which should stop the game being closed by Android during long sessions.

## 2026-11-27 | Safer saves

change: Battles are about a third shorter: every hit, heal, shield and damage-over-time is stronger, so a typical fight lasts around 8 rounds.

new: A one-time tip explains Weakness Break the first time you see a foe Broken.
