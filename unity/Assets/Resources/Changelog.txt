# Sigilbound "What's new" (player-facing; newest first). Tags: new, fix, change, balance.
# Keep lines short and friendly; this is shown in the game after each update.
# Start a NEW "## date | title" entry for each batch of changes (the game remembers which entries were seen).

## 2026-11-27 | Safer updates
fix: The in-game updater now checks that a downloaded update is complete before installing it, on Android and Windows.

## 2026-11-27 | Smaller download
change: The game download is about 30 MB smaller: hero models are packed more tightly, with no visible difference.

## 2026-11-27 | Lighter on memory
fix: The game no longer keeps every hero you have seen in memory: heroes not on screen are let go (the last few stay for quick reuse), which should stop the game being closed by Android during long sessions.
fix: Hero textures use about half the memory they did, and small leaks during long auto-replay sessions are cleaned up after each battle.
fix: A hero whose model failed to load once now gets another try instead of staying a block figure.

## 2026-11-27 | Safer saves
fix: If your save ever can't be read, the game no longer starts over on top of it: the damaged save is kept, the backup from your previous save is loaded, and nothing is saved over your progress until you choose.
fix: The game now keeps a backup of your previous save.
fix: Heroes from a newer version of the game are kept in your save instead of being removed by an older version.

## 2026-11-27 | Faster, deeper battles
change: Battles are about a third shorter: every hit, heal, shield and damage-over-time is stronger, so a typical fight lasts around 8 rounds.
new: Weakness Break: hit a foe three times with the element that beats it and it is Broken, losing its next turn. Bosses take twice as long to break; the Titan can't be broken.
new: A one-time tip explains Weakness Break the first time you see a foe Broken.
new: Poison and Burn stack up to 3 times; each stack ticks. The Titan doesn't stack them, so Titan damage and rewards stay the same.
change: Speed matters more: attackers and supports are faster, tanks and bruisers slower, and the fastest unit usually acts first.
change: Critical hits can now land on an element disadvantage, at half the usual chance (not against the Titan).
change: Auto battle opens with team-wide heal and shield skills when they help, not only when someone is hurt.
balance: Stage 14-4 eased so it fits between its neighbours again.
balance: 39 heroes retuned for the new pace; Vex's King's Command and Crown of Ruin cooldowns swapped; Obrecht, Ondessa, Morvoren and Solenne's taunt skills trimmed.

## 2026-11-27 | Sharper battles
change: Long fights now Enrage from round 10: damage rises and healing and shields shrink each round, so no battle can stall forever.
change: Shields are 20% smaller.
change: Auto battle is smarter: it no longer casts cleanses, shields or buffs that would do nothing, and finishes off enemies it can kill.
balance: Poison and Burn on campaign bosses are capped like on the Titan, so they no longer melt bosses.
balance: 22 heroes retuned for the new rules.

## 2026-11-27 | The storm coast, painted
new: Act II has its own painted map, from Saltreach harbour past the fens, barrows and ember groves to the Hollow King's spire.

## 2026-11-27 | Painted portraits
new: Every hero now has a hand-painted portrait card.

## 2026-11-27 | A painted world
new: The Act I campaign map is now a hand-painted world map, with every chapter on its own land from the western woods to the corrupted east.
new: The summon altar glows behind your shards, and the citadel greets new players on the title screen.
new: Painted icons for gold, shards and essence, the menu bar and the battle modes, ribbon banners on screen titles, and bronze frames on hero rows.

## 2026-11-27 | Gilded menus
new: Painted blue buttons, gem chips for tabs and filters, and ornate gold frames around panels and cards.

## 2026-11-27 | New music
new: Boss stages have their own dark battle theme, with war drums, low brass and a choir. It starts as the lair reveal ends.
new: Act II battles have a stormy coastal theme with a fiddle and frame drum.
new: The campaign map has its own calm theme.

## 2026-11-27 | Arriving at the lair
